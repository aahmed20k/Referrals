Questions from patients.

What happens after my doctor sends a referral?

We review it, verify your insurance, and call you to schedule. If anything is missing, we get it from your doctor's office directly.

Will I be treated at home or somewhere else?

It depends on your medication, your health, and your insurance. Your prescriber and our team decide together, and the setting can change later if needed.

How should I prepare for my first infusion?
  • Confirm the date, time, location, and how long it will take.
  • Have your medication and allergy list ready.
  • Ask whether to eat, drink, or take your usual medications beforehand.
  • Wear clothing that gives easy access to your arm or line.
  • Arrange a ride if your care team recommends it.

Always follow the specific instructions from your prescriber or nurse.

Will my insurance cover it?

We check your benefits and handle prior authorization before treatment starts, and tell you what we know about your cost. Your health plan makes the final coverage decision.

Who do I call with a question during treatment?

Call us at (513) 780-5880. For a medical emergency, call 911.
